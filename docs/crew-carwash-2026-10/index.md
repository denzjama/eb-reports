*The job ads are honest and full of facts, but the reasons to join rest on a Glassdoor ranking, “Industry-leading training” and “full potential”, and none of them shows a candidate where the job leads.*

**Employer Brand Score — 64 out of 100** · coverage 100% · high reliability

- The job ads give the facts most needed to decide: hourly pay or salary range, daily pay, which benefits are full-time only, minimum weekly hours and what the work demands of your body. This is the strongest part of the material.
- The reasons to choose Crew are a Glassdoor ranking, “Industry-leading training”, “full potential” and a fun team. None of the material explains what the ranking was based on. Only training comes with any substance: the careers page lists what the training covers.
- Growth is promised in every ad and in the June 2026 press release, but no page shows where a customer service job or the Fast Track Manager role leads, or how many people have made that move.
- The best proof sits in the wrong place. The careers page lists leadership training, including running a business and manager coaching. The Fast Track Manager ad, the one role where this matters most, says only “Industry-leading training”.
- Old and current pages disagree on details. A Maple Grove page last changed in December 2022 offers a sign-on bonus, tuition help of up to $3,500 a year and applications through a different system. Current pages mention none of these.

## What works

**The ads state hard facts, with their conditions.** The customer service ads give “$15 - $16 per hour + incentive pay”, daily pay options at no cost, and paid time off plus 6 paid holidays marked as full-time only. They also give minimum hours of 10 a week part-time and 30 full-time (20 part-time for the daytime role). The manager ad gives a salary range of $45,000 to $60,000 plus incentive pay. Conditions such as full-time only are kept next to the benefit they limit.

**The physical reality of the job is stated plainly.** Every ad lists standing for up to 8+ hours a day, working near moving mechanical parts, climbing ladders and scaffolds, lifting at least 25 pounds and “Be able to withstand extended exposure to all weather extremes”. A candidate can judge fit before applying.

**The customer service job is described as it is done.** The Indiana role page explains the two parts of the job: advising customers on wash options and directing vehicles onto the conveyor. One associate describes a shift rotating between service advisor, loader and lot checks.

**Training has content.** The careers page lists what the training covers at no cost: hands-on management, customer service and sales, car wash technical skills, leadership, running a business, manager coaching, and safety and CPR certifications. It is one of the two promises in the material with meaningful support.

## The main problems

**Growth is promised everywhere and shown nowhere.** Growth is the most repeated promise after the award, and the only evidence offered is new locations. New locations show the company is growing; they say nothing about what happens to a person's job. For the Fast Track Manager role the missing path is the missing core of the offer.

> Every ad promises the chance to achieve your full potential; the manager ad lists “Incredible growth potential” among Crew's commitments.

**The reasons to join rest on a ranking and stock phrases whose basis is not shown.** The ranking is specific and dated, but no page says what it measured or what it means for someone washing cars in Greenfield. “Industry-leading” compares Crew with an industry the material never describes. Take these away and what remains is pay, hours and a list of benefits.

> “Recognized as one of Glassdoor’s Best Places to Work for multiple years and ranked #1 in the nation in 2026, Crew Carwash is dedicated to creating a positive and fast-paced work environment.” (press release)

**The founder's line is the one memorable idea, and nothing shows what it means.** The line ties back to the founder and to 1948, which no other employer can claim. The material never gives a single practice that makes staff feel Crew is in the people business. Daily pay, role rotation and safety certification are real practices, but they sit elsewhere and are never linked to the line.

> Every ad repeats: “We're really in the people business, we just happen to wash cars!”

**“Flexible schedules” is never defined, and the manager ad puts it next to a fixed five-day rotation.** The material does not say what can be flexed: days, shift times, or total hours. For the manager role the stated requirement leaves little visible room. A candidate reading this may reasonably ask which of the two statements describes the job.

> “Flexible schedules” is a commitment in all three ads.

## Where to start

1. Publish the growth path from associate to Fast Track Manager and beyond, with real numbers.
2. Replace “Industry-leading training” and the bare award claim with what the training covers and what the ranking measured.
3. Update or remove the 2022 Maple Grove page, and spell out what “Flexible schedules” means for each role.

## About Employer Brand Auditor

Employer Brand Auditor is a free, open-source plugin for Claude Code. It reads an employer brand the way a candidate does: what the company promises, what backs it up, and what sets it apart from other employers.

**How to install.** You need [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code). Run two commands in a terminal:

```
claude plugin marketplace add denzjama/employer-brand-auditor
claude plugin install employer-brand-auditor@employer-brand-auditor
```

**How to use it.** Ask Claude Code to "audit [company name]'s employer brand", or put your own material in the folder: an EVP, job ads, a careers page. For a fuller study with social channels and employee reviews, ask for a deep audit.

**What you get.** An HTML report with a 0–100 score across eight dimensions, a quotation behind every finding, the main problems, an action plan and rewritten copy. The method is explained inside the report. Code and docs: [github.com/denzjama/employer-brand-auditor](https://github.com/denzjama/employer-brand-auditor).
