# Employer Brand Reviews

Evidence-first reviews of how well-known employers talk to candidates: careers sites, job ads, employee stories and official social channels. Every review is made with the open-source [Employer Brand Auditor](https://github.com/denzjama/employer-brand-auditor) and links to the full report with its method and sources.

The reviews assess public communication only, not what working at a company is like.
